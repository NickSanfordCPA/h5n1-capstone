# X post topic labels — labeling guide (v1, 2026-09-28)

One label per post. Pick the label for what the post is **mainly** about. Precedence when a
post fits two: `local_observation` > `outbreak_report` > the rest.

| label | Use when the post... | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `local_observation` | reports something the author (or someone near them) **saw firsthand**: sick/dead wild birds, sick or dying poultry, their own flock/herd, a neighbor's farm, a local closure — **not** relaying an official announcement | "found 3 dead geese at the pond by my house", "lost half my hens this week", "neighbor's turkey barn is quarantined" |
| `outbreak_report` | relays a detection, confirmation, case count, or quarantine from officials or news (animal or human, any country) | "USDA confirms HPAI in Meeker County", "2nd human case in Missouri" |
| `policy_response` | is about government/industry response: culling/depopulation policy, indemnity, testing mandates, vaccination policy, trade bans, agency criticism | "USDA should vaccinate layers", "culling millions of birds is insane" |
| `economic` | is about prices, supply, jobs, or business losses (eggs, turkey, dairy, poultry industry) | "$7 eggs", "egg shortage", "farm lost its whole contract" |
| `human_health` | is about risk to people: pandemic potential, human symptoms, raw milk, worker safety, human vaccines | "is bird flu the next pandemic?", "don't drink raw milk" |
| `science` | explains or discusses virology, mutation, transmission research, surveillance methods | "PB2 E627K mutation found", "new study on mammal transmission" |
| `off_topic` | is not really about avian influenza: jokes, unrelated "flu", spam, crypto, conspiracy with no H5N1 content, or unintelligible | "farm flu KO'd him", "$FLU token" |

Notes
- A post about a *different country* keeps its topic (e.g. a Cambodian human case is
  `outbreak_report`). Geography is handled separately.
- Opinion attached to news ("USDA confirms another farm, this is terrifying") is
  `outbreak_report` unless the opinion is the main content.
- Conspiracy / misinformation that is about H5N1 itself goes to the topic it discusses
  (usually `human_health` or `policy_response`), not `off_topic`.
